How an assessment works

From incident coordinates to operational clearance guidance in three automated steps.

STEP 01 Input

Incident Context

Operator pins the incident location via address search or on-scene device GPS, and confirms the local timestamp.

Coordinates · Date · Solar Hour
STEP 02 Automated

Telemetry Ingestion

The system queries historical or live atmospheric records for that exact coordinate and hour without manual lookup.

Precipitation · Wind · Visibility
STEP 03 ML Model

Severity Inference

A class-balanced Random Forest classifier evaluates compound risks to estimate traffic disruption severity and standard clearance protocols.

Tiers 1–4 · Clearance SOP · Probabilities
Output Matrix

Predicted traffic impact tiers

Standard clearance windows and response priorities mapped to model predictions.

Level 1 < 15 min clearance

Minimal Disruption

Shoulder or curb incident with negligible lane obstruction. Traffic flows smoothly; routine clearance protocol.

Level 2 15 – 45 min clearance

Minor Delays

Single travel lane affected. Moderate traffic queue forms; localized advisory warnings recommended.

Level 3 45 – 90 min clearance

Significant Congestion

Multiple lanes blocked on major corridors or highways. Substantial queueing; alternate route diversions triggered.

Level 4 90+ min clearance

Critical Corridor Shutdown

Complete corridor gridlock, frequently compounded by adverse weather (rain, fog) or high-speed junction geometries. Emergency clearance priority.
