Workflow Pipeline

How an assessment works

From incident coordinates to operational clearance guidance in three automated steps.

STEP 01 Input

Incident Context

Operator pins the incident location via address search or on-scene device GPS, and confirms the local timestamp.

Coordinates · Date · Solar Hour
STEP 02 Automated

Telemetry Ingestion

The system queries historical or live atmospheric records for that exact coordinate and hour without manual lookup.

Precipitation · Wind · Visibility
STEP 03 ML Model

Severity Inference & Dispatch Queue

A class-balanced Random Forest classifier evaluates compound risks, persists the incident to the TOC Command Queue, and tracks role-based clearance workflows.

Output Matrix

Predicted traffic impact tiers

Standard clearance windows and response priorities mapped to model predictions.

Level 1 10 – 25 min clearance

Minor Delay

Routine shoulder clearance with negligible lane obstruction. No mainline lane closures required; LOW dispatch priority.

Level 2 25 – 45 min clearance

Moderate Traffic Disruption

Single travel lane affected. Dispatch standard traffic patrol unit and monitor corridor camera feeds; MEDIUM dispatch priority.

Level 3 45 – 90 min clearance

Significant Traffic Backlog

Multiple lanes affected on major corridors. Activate Variable Message Signs (VMS) and reroute upcoming transit; HIGH dispatch priority.

Level 4 90+ min clearance

Critical Corridor Shutdown

Severe corridor gridlock, often compounded by adverse weather or junction geometries. Dispatch heavy-duty recovery cranes & trigger regional detours; CRITICAL priority.
