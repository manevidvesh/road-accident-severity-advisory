What goes into an assessment

Incident Context

  • Location Address / GPS
  • Month 1 – 12
  • Solar Hour 00:00 – 23:00

Environmental Conditions

  • Temperature °F
  • Humidity %
  • Visibility Statute miles
  • Wind speed mph
  • Precipitation Inches
  • Weather / Solar WMO / Day-Night

Road Infrastructure

  • Pedestrian crossing Yes / No
  • Highway junction Yes / No
  • Traffic signal Yes / No
